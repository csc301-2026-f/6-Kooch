# Kooch Interactive Mockups

Our D1 mockup consists of two complementary interactive prototypes. Together, they demonstrate both the newcomer-facing settlement journey and the Kooch administrator workflow for creating and maintaining journeys.

## 1. Newcomer Roadmap and Help Experience

**Figma:** [Open the newcomer roadmap prototype](https://www.figma.com/design/k2P7XKlHIP47uJe29GTYsy)

This prototype presents the user-facing experience, including:

- Starting the onboarding quiz
- Identifying the user's current settlement situation
- Reviewing the user's situation before generating a roadmap
- Viewing a personalized roadmap and current journey stage
- Understanding available and blocked tasks
- Viewing task deadlines and prerequisites
- Requesting contextual help from within a journey task
- Using Find Help without first creating a journey
- Viewing multilingual and right-to-left roadmap content

This prototype primarily demonstrates **US1, US2, US3, US6, and US7** from `Planning.md`.

## 2. Admin Journey Creation and Editing

**Figma prototype:** [Open the admin journey prototype](https://www.figma.com/proto/pKfe6CNrnbx1mNWCVgZjvY/Kooch--Journey-Update-Design?node-id=11-3345&p=f&t=yLJcK3nsr1Wsgg99-1&scaling=min-zoom&content-scaling=fixed&page-id=0%3A1&starting-point-node-id=11%3A3345&show-proto-sidebar=1)

**Prototype password:** `TeamBanana123`

**Design notes:** [Open the supporting notes](https://anotepad.com/notes/j5x5j3fp)

This prototype demonstrates the Kooch administrator workflow for creating and editing journeys from the admin panel. It focuses on allowing Kooch's content team to maintain journey content and rules without requiring a developer for every update.

This prototype primarily demonstrates **US4 and US5** from `Planning.md`.

## Scope and Status

These are first-version interactive mockups created for Deliverable 1. They do not include a backend or persistent data. The designs may change during development based on additional partner feedback, technical constraints, and usability findings.

Both prototypes were created by the student team and reviewed with Kooch to establish a shared understanding of the proposed product experience.
